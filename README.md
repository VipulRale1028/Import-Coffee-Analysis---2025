# Import-Coffee-Analysis---2025
1️⃣ Coffee imports in 2025 are huge

The country bought $239 million worth of coffee.

Total coffee quantity imported is 54.58 million KG.

This means coffee demand is strong.

2️⃣ Coffee comes from many countries

Coffee was imported from 33 countries.

Big suppliers include USA, Brazil, Colombia, India, Indonesia, Uganda, Peru, Kenya, and others.

This shows the supply chain is very global.

3️⃣ Africa gives the highest value of coffee

Africa → $57M (biggest contributor)

Asia → $35M

LATAM (Latin America) → $17M

North America → $2M

Europe → $1M

Africa is the strongest region for coffee imports.

4️⃣ Monthly quantity keeps changing

Highest month: October (7.8M KG)

Lowest month: November (1.3M KG)

Good months: Jan, Feb, Mar, May, Jun

Slow months: Jul, Aug

This helps plan stock and sales.

5️⃣ Coffee prices also change every month

Lowest price: Jan ($3.6/kg)

Highest price: Aug ($5.5/kg)

Prices rise slowly from Jan → May, fall in Jun → Jul, then rise again at the end.

This helps decide when to buy more or less.

6️⃣ CIF Value also fluctuates monthly

Highest value: Oct ($35M) → High imports + high demand

Lowest value: Nov ($6M) → Low import activity

Stable months: Jan, Feb, Mar, Jun, Jul

This shows when import business is strong/weak.

7️⃣ Top importers (companies)

CCL Products India Ltd → $73M (market leader)

Indus Coffee Pvt Ltd → $34M

Vayhan Coffee Ltd → $34M

Tata Consumer Products Ltd → $28M

CCL dominates the market and brings the most business.

8️⃣ Average price per KG

Overall average price is $4.37/kg.

This is useful for planning profit margins.

9️⃣ Market Diversity

49 importers in the ecosystem.

This means high competition but also strong distribution.

🌟 Final Sales-Level Summary (Short)

Coffee imports in 2025 show strong demand, with $239M in value coming from 33 countries. Africa leads as the top region, and October is the highest import month. Prices fluctuate, peaking in August, while CCL Products is the biggest importer. Overall, the insights give a complete picture of market size, supplier strength, price trends, and importer contribution—good for sales planning and forecasting.
